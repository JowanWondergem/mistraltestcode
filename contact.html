Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the gallery?

Simply visit the Home page and browse the images. No login is required. The images change automatically every day.

Can I download the images?

All images are provided by Unsplash and are free to use under the Unsplash License. You can download them directly from Unsplash.

Why do the images change daily?

The gallery uses a daily rotation system to provide fresh content every day. This creates a dynamic experience where you'll see new images each time you visit.

Can I contribute images?

Currently, images are sourced from Unsplash. If you're a photographer and would like your work featured, consider contributing to Unsplash where our images are sourced from.

Is there a mobile app?

Not at this time. However, our website is fully responsive and works great on mobile devices. You can save it to your home screen for app-like access.
